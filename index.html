Hello There!

I connect business systems, databases and reporting tools with AI so that teams spend less time on manual data work. Most of what I build follows one pattern: find the repetitive process (a report compiled by hand, an inbox answered one email at a time, a form nobody has time to read), connect the systems behind it, and automate it so the right people get the data faster.

I have 4+ years of experience across software development, data science and AI automation. Day to day I work with n8n, Python, SQL, Google Apps Script, Looker Studio and Power BI, and with the Claude API, LangChain and vector databases for the AI side. I'm tool-agnostic: I pick what fits the stack a team already has.

I also teach. At 10Alytics I design data science and AI curriculum, which means I'm used to explaining technical work clearly to non-technical stakeholders.
